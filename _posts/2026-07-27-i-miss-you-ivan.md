I miss you too. And perhaps the hardest thing right now is that I can't figure out what I want. I constantly catch myself wanting to text you about something trivial, send you a funny video, tell you what happened today, ask how you are. But then I stop myself, because I don't know if I have the right to do that and if it won't only make things more painful for us. I don't know if I'll be able to communicate with you the same way now. But I know how much I miss it. I miss our late-night conversations, games that turned into little memories, movies together, discussing every little detail, our strange ideas and dreams. I even miss those moments when we were simply silent, but I knew you were somewhere nearby. I want to hear about your day again. I want to know how your family is, what's going on in your life, what makes you happy or sad right now, who you're spending time with. I truly care. It never ceased to be important to me. Even when we barely spoke, I still thought about you, whether everything was okay with you

That's probably why it's so hard for me. Because my feelings for you haven't disappeared, but along with them have come fear, confusion, and a feeling that I no longer understand what's right. Sometimes I feel like I need to let go. Sometimes I feel like I want to fight for us. And sometimes I just want to recapture, even if just for one evening, that feeling when there wasn't this heaviness between us and we could be together without the fear of saying something wrong. I don't even want to go back to what already happened. We both know how much pain it caused. I want to talk not only about what we lost, but also about what still remains. Because, despite everything, you are still very dear to me. And that hasn't changed. You wrote that change is possible, and I really want to believe that. But right now, I can't honestly tell you what exactly I want from the future. I don't want to promise anything I'm not sure of myself. The only thing I'm absolutely sure of is that you haven't become a stranger to me. You're still the person I love, the person I care about, and the person I miss terribly. Sometimes I feel like I miss more than just our relationship. I miss you as a person. The way you laughed, the way you shared your thoughts, the way you could suddenly start talking about philosophy or some trivial matter that would suddenly turn into a long conversation. I miss the feeling of being able to share everything with you—from the silliest jokes to the deepest thoughts. And perhaps that's what makes everything so confusing. Because my heart reaches out to you, but my mind is still trying to figure out how to keep us from hurting each other again. I don't know what will happen next. But I really want that, no matter what our path turns out to be, we don't lose that part of us that was always real. Because you are one of the most important people in my life. And no matter how much time passes, that will remain true

I don't sleep well lately and I can't stop thinking about you 



I love you Ivan
