All these days, I've been trying to collect my thoughts, but each time I realized I didn't know where to begin. I guess I want to explain to you what's going on with me first, because I think you deserve to know what's going on inside me



Honestly, since I returned, I've felt like I've lost myself. I wake up, do some chores around the house, do little things, but it all seems automatic. I've lost interest in almost everything. I don't want to go out, meet people, or even talk. Sometimes I catch myself staring at the ceiling, wondering how another day went by. And the worst part is, I feel like I'm wasting my best years. I know I could change something, I know what I'd like to do, but I have neither the strength nor the desire to take the first step. As for us I think about us constantly. Not a day goes by that I don't think about everything that happened. But I think about more than just us. I endlessly replay your family, my family, all the conversations, the most painful words, the hardest moments in my head. And every time, it overwhelms me again. Sometimes I just want to sit down and cry, because I can't stop regretting so much



I constantly think about what your parents think of me now, what my family thinks. I feel like all of this will stay between us forever. And I'll be honest with you: I really don't know what to do next. Even if one day everything miraculously gets better, I can't imagine looking your family in the eyes. I can't imagine talking to my family about you calmly. I feel like I just won't find the words. I used to tell you that none of this matters, that the most important thing is us. And I really meant it then. But now I increasingly catch myself thinking that maybe that's where it all ended. Not because I stopped loving you. Not at all. But because some things change people so deeply that it's impossible to pretend nothing happened



I don't know what to do and even if we get back together, I'm afraid I'll never be able to feel the same lightness again. I'm afraid to live with this burden inside me. I'm afraid that over time, reproaches, resentments, memories will arise between us that will return again and again. I don't want us to start hurting each other even more just because we couldn't let go of the past. But at the same time, the thought of losing you scares me just as much. Because with you, I feel like I'm losing a huge part of myself. I'm losing our dreams, everything I imagined when I thought about the future. Sometimes I feel like after you, I'll never be able to be as genuine and happy with someone again. I'm afraid that you really will be that one love, after which everything else will seem like a feeble attempt to forget what happened between us



I'm not writing all this to put an end to it. On the contrary. I simply don't want to remain silent any longer and pretend that everything is fine with me. It's really important for me to hear from you. Not just "everything will be fine," but what you really think. How do you see us in a few months? Do you even believe in our future? What do you think we can do to avoid losing each other completely? Or maybe you, too, feel like we've reached a dead end? I want to hear the truth, whatever it is, because uncertainty is hurting me the most right now. And, getting back to your question from the letter: no, I haven't met anyone. And, to be honest, I hardly communicate with anyone right now, even my close friends. I spend most of my time with my family or just watching movies to take my mind off all this for a little while. I don't know if you still visit my Instagram sometimes, but I've almost stopped even going there. I used to be able to scroll through my feed for hours, but now I open the app once every few days and close it almost immediately. It's like I don't care anymore. Perhaps what I want most right now is not to bring back the past, but the feeling that we still have a future. Because despite all the pain, despite all the doubts and fears, hope still lives inside me. Very small, very fragile, but it's still there. And perhaps that's why I'm writing you this letter. Because if I didn't care, I would have stopped searching for words long ago
